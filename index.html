How It Works

5 steps. Fully automated.
Runs without you.

From the moment a lead fills a form to the moment your agent walks into a qualified call every step is handled automatically.

01
Lead Capture

Lead enquires the system receives instantly

A lead fills out a form, sends a DM, or clicks a CTA on Instagram, Facebook, a website, or an ad. The system receives their contact in real time via direct integration. No manual checking. No delay. No leads slipping through at night.

02
Instant Response

Personalised reply in 30–60 seconds

The AI sends a warm, personalised opening message that acknowledges the enquiry and opens a structured conversation within 30 to 60 seconds of first contact. At 11PM. On a Sunday. Every single time.

03
Lead Qualification

5 qualifying questions all automated

The agent works through a defined qualification flow: buy, sell, or rent? Budget range? Preferred location? Property type? Timeline? Every answer is captured and stored so your agent walks in already knowing the lead inside out.

04
Conversation & Objection Handling

Answers questions. Keeps the lead engaged.

The AI handles common queries about pricing, availability, process, and next steps. It manages hesitations and objections keeping the lead warm and guiding every conversation toward one outcome: a confirmed appointment.

05
Appointment Booking

Qualified. Booked. Summary sent to your agent.

Once a lead qualifies and is ready, the system offers available slots and confirms the booking directly in your calendar. The agent receives a complete lead summary name, source, budget, timeline, property preference before every single call.
